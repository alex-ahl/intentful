# Intentful Support

Questions, problems or feedback: email **intentful.support@gmail.com**.

## What Intentful does

When you open an app you've chosen, Intentful shows a screen asking
**"Are you sure?"**

- **Start 1-min wait** closes the app. Open it again after a minute and tap
  **Unlock for 15 min**; the app closes once more, and the next time you open it
  you're in for 15 minutes.
- **Not now** just closes the app.
- **Cancel wait** drops a wait that's running.

## Common questions

**The "Are you sure?" screen doesn't appear.**
Open Intentful and check that **Ask before opening** is on and at least one app
is selected. Selected apps have dimmed icons on the home screen.

**Intentful says it needs Screen Time access.**
Go to **Settings → Screen Time** and make sure Screen Time is turned on, then
open Intentful and tap **Grant Screen Time access**.

**The app closes after I tap a button.**
That's intended. Open the app again to continue.

**Other apps still ask during my 15 minutes.**
Only the app you unlocked is let through; the rest keep asking.

**How do I turn it off?**
Open Intentful and turn off **Ask before opening**. Deleting the app also
removes every shield.
