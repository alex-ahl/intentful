# Privacy Policy

**Intentful collects no data.**

There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no
backend. The app makes no network requests.

## What the app stores

Everything stays on your iPhone:

- **Which apps you chose.** Stored by Apple's Screen Time APIs as opaque tokens
  inside the app's App Group container. The tokens are meaningless outside your
  device, and the app itself cannot read which apps they refer to — only how
  many.
- **Whether the feature is on.** A single true/false value in the same
  container.

Deleting the app deletes both.

## Screen Time data

Intentful uses Apple's Family Controls and DeviceActivity frameworks to shield
the apps you pick. Any app usage information these frameworks expose stays on
the device, is used only to shield and unshield your chosen apps, and is never
transmitted, shared, or sold.

## Children

Intentful is for the device owner managing their own apps. It is not a parental
control product and has no supervision or reporting features.
